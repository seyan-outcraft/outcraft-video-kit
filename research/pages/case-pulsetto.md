## Challenges: Simplifying Biohacking & Overcoming the Premium Friction

Pulsetto exists to solve one of the most widespread challenges of modern life: chronic stress, anxiety, and poor sleep quality. Operating in the cutting-edge wellness space, Pulsetto manufactures a non-invasive vagus nerve stimulator (VNS) wearable. By delivering gentle, scientifically calibrated electrical impulses to the neck, the device stimulates the parasympathetic nervous system—specifically triggering the body's natural "rest and digest" state in under 10 minutes.

Because Pulsetto’s neuromodulation device is a premium, high-consideration biohacking product, it faces a unique set of consumer bottlenecks:

- **The Need for Education:** Prospective customers frequently require high-touch reassurance regarding how vagus nerve stimulation works, its safety profile, and why a premium hardware investment is justified.
- **Cart Abandonment at Checkout:** Traditional checkout funnels suffer from significant drop-offs. Hesitant shoppers often leave their carts right before purchasing, representing a massive loss in paid acquisition spend.
- **Untapped Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):** Once a customer purchases a Pulsetto, there is a prime, yet often missed, opportunity to follow up with secondary accessories, app upgrades, or premium wellness programs.

To solve this, Pulsetto needed an automated, conversational, and deeply personalized way to educate leads, recover lost checkouts, and maximize customer lifetime value without exponentially scaling their physical support and sales teams.

## Solution: Hyper-Personalized, Multi-Channel AI Outreach

Pulsetto integrated Outcraft AI to build a seamless safety net for their sales pipeline. On top of relying only on static, easily ignored emails, Pulsetto launched two highly proactive conversational campaigns managed entirely by Outcraft's AI sales agents.

By deploying multi-channel outreach, the AI agent dynamically engages customers at critical inflection points:

- Real-Time Abandoned Checkout Recovery: When a potential buyer drops off, the AI agent immediately schedules a friendly outreach attempt. It connects with prospects through their preferred channels—utilizing natural, human-like voice calls to answer technical questions and clear up checkout friction.

- Post-Purchase Upsell & Support: Within days of receiving their device, customers are contacted by the AI to offer helpful tips on using the Pulsetto app and introduce complementary premium add-ons, turning one-time buyers into active, long-term brand advocates.


Outcraft AI handles this massive scale autonomously, delivering consistent, top-tier service to tens of thousands of global shoppers simultaneously.
