## Challenges: Conveying Premium Value & Maximizing Customer Lifetime Value

Taima exists to solve a serious but often overlooked problem in modern kitchens: hidden toxins and unsafe materials in everyday cookware and kitchenware. Most conventional cookware is manufactured using chemical coatings, mixed metals, or low-grade materials that can degrade over time and contaminate food. For example, common non-stick coated pans rely on PFAS and synthetic chemical coatings.

To solve this, Taima manufactures pure titanium cookware that offers a stable, non-toxic cooking surface, extreme heat resistance, and lifetime durability without chipping, flaking, or peeling.

Because these items are premium investments, cart abandonment and maximizing customer lifetime value are critical challenges. Every abandoned cart represents not just lost revenue, but wasted acquisition costs. Taima needed a proactive way to re-engage hesitant shoppers to recover lost sales, while simultaneously nurturing first-time buyers into loyal, repeat customers to maximize their marketing efficiency.

## Solution: AI-Powered Outreach with Outcraft AI

To tackle checkout drop-offs, Taima Titanium launched an Abandoned Cart campaign utilizing Outcraft AI. The AI agent served as an automated conversational assistant to win back hesitant shoppers.

Outcraft AI initiated highly effective campaigns with a multi-channel outreach strategy. Instead of relying on a single touchpoint, the AI dynamically engaged leads through their preferred channels:

- Calls accounted for the majority of outreach at 69%.

- SMS text messaging accounted for **26%** of the volume.

- Emails made up the remaining 5% of the initial outreach strategy.

## Impressive Results

To tackle these challenges, Taima Titanium launched two comprehensive campaigns utilizing Outcraft AI as an automated conversational assistant.

- Real-Time Abandoned Checkout Recovery Taima launched a multi-channel Abandoned Cart campaign to win back hesitant shoppers.
  - **Recovered Revenue:** The AI abandoned cart campaign generated over $180,000 in total revenue.
  - **High Conversion:** The campaign achieved an impressive conversion rate of over 29%.
  - **Strong Engagement:** Leads showed a nearly 33% engagement rate, with over 26% of engaged prospects replying positively.
- Post-Purchase Upsell Calls To maximize customer lifetime value, Taima Titanium also implemented a post-purchase campaign with the AI agent proactively engaging recent buyers.
  - **Added Upsell Revenue:** The post-purchase outreach generated nearly $42,000 in additional total revenue.
  - **High Engagement Rates:** The upsell campaign saw a highly active customer base, yielding an engagement rate of over 33%.
  - **Positive Customer Sentiment:** Among those who engaged with the post-purchase outreach, nearly 22% replied positively.
