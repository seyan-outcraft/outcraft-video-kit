## FAQ

Find quick answers to your most common questions below.

What is Outcraft AI?


Outcraft AI is an autonomous revenue engine that drives growth across the entire customer lifecycle. It deploys real-time, human-like voice AI alongside coordinated follow-up across calls, SMS, email, and WhatsApp, and decides which channel to use for each customer automatically. B2B SaaS and e-commerce teams use Outcraft AI to follow up with leads in seconds, recover lost revenue, and convert more customers without adding headcount.

How is Outcraft AI different from Bland, Synthflow, and Retell?


Outcraft AI is a full revenue engine, not just a voice-AI builder. Where tools like Bland, Synthflow, and Retell give you an AI phone agent to configure, Outcraft AI runs the entire workflow end to end - qualifying the lead, choosing between voice, SMS, email, and WhatsApp, booking the meeting, and following up - across 20+ native integrations. You get outcomes like booked demos, recovered carts, and converted trials, not a toolkit you have to assemble.

What does Outcraft AI integrate with?


Outcraft AI integrates natively with 20+ tools, including Salesforce, HubSpot, Shopify, and Stripe. It plugs into your existing CRM and e-commerce stack so it can act on real-time signals - a new inbound lead, an abandoned checkout, a failed payment, an expiring trial - and book meetings straight into your calendar. There’s no rip-and-replace; Outcraft AI works on top of the stack you already run.

Can Outcraft AI hold a real phone conversation, or does it just read a script?


Outcraft AI holds real, natural phone conversations - it does not just read a script. Its real-time voice AI understands intonation, handles objections, and adapts to what the customer actually says, in 40+ languages and regional dialects. Customers are routinely surprised it’s an AI agent. It can qualify leads, answer product questions, handle refunds and cancellations, and book meetings live on the call.

How fast can Outcraft AI follow up with a new lead?


Outcraft AI follows up with inbound leads in seconds - within the first few minutes, where conversion rates are highest. The moment a lead fills out a form or downloads a lead magnet, Outcraft AI can call them back, text them, or email them automatically, then keep the conversation going across channels until the lead is qualified and a meeting is booked. Speed-to-lead is one of its core advantages over human teams.
