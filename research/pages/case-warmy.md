Warmy chose Outcraft to ensure every trial signup gets immediate, personalized outreach. ## How Outcraft AI Boosted Warmy’s Trial Conversions

Once integrated via the simple HubSpot CRM App, Outcraft AI began engaging each new trial user within minutes of account creation. The AI agent automatically calls the user about 5 minutes after signup to introduce Warmy, answer initial questions, and offer to schedule a demo, if the prospect agrees to have a demo call, then Outcraft AI books the time and sends the calendar invite while still on the call with the prospect. If the prospect doesn’t pick up the phone, Outcraft seamlessly switches channels – sending a friendly welcome email and an SMS text, so the lead is reached on their preferred medium. Over the next days, the AI continues the conversation via calls, email, or text, handling common questions and nudging the trial user toward booking a meeting. This multi-channel, rapid follow-up strategy ensured 100% of Warmy’s trial sign-ups received timely outreach. The AI never forgets to follow up – it can politely persist with additional emails or texts in the following days, something human reps might struggle to do consistently. Importantly, Outcraft’s AI also qualifies the leads during these interactions, so when a meeting is booked, it’s with a genuinely interested, appropriate prospect. Warmy’s team configured the AI’s messaging to fit their brand’s tone, but from there, the entire lead engagement process ran on autopilot.

Switching to Outcraft AI for trial follow-up has delivered impressive results for Warmy. Immediately, the number of weekly demo calls skyrocketed from 3–5 to about 18–22 on average. This is roughly a 4–5× increase in weekly sales meetings, filling the sales calendar with qualified demos. Many of these meetings were booked outside of normal working hours or after multiple touches – opportunities that would have been missed without an always-on AI agent.

Crucially, Warmy also observed about a 15% uplift in the conversion rate of trial users to scheduled demos since implementing Outcraft AI. Every trial user now gets the white-glove treatment of a prompt call, email, and text,

It’s like having an elite SDR team working 24/7, at a fraction of the cost of hiring additional staff. Warmy’s success story illustrates just how effective rapid, AI-driven lead engagement can be.
