# Outcraft AI — research notes (from outcraft.ai, 2026-08-29)

Source: public website. Login-only app UI not captured yet.

## One line
An AI sales team that calls, texts, emails and WhatsApps every inbound
signal within minutes — until it books, buys, or hands over with context.

## The signals it acts on
A form fill. A demo request. A missed call. An abandoned cart.
A failed payment. A trial signup. A demo no-show.

## Four jobs (site's own words)
- Convert inbound leads
- Activate users
- Prevent churn
- Recover lost revenue

## The three claims that matter
1. **Speed.** Minutes, not days.
2. **Real conversation.** Voice AI handles the objection live. 40+ languages.
   "Most don't realise it's AI until you tell them."
3. **Decides, not sends.** It reads the reply, picks the channel, picks the
   next move. Every branch is an outcome, not a scheduled send.

## The enemy (positioning)
Bland, Synthflow, Retell, Vapi = a voice agent you assemble.
- They: one channel, you script the flow, you write the glue code,
  success = call minutes.
- Outcraft: whole workflow, 4 channels, 20+ native integrations,
  success = booked demos, recovered carts, converted trials.
