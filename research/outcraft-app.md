## Design language (useful for video 2)
- Light UI. Near-white page. White cards. Soft 1px borders. ~12px radius.
- Left sidebar, dark logo, purple active state on the nav item.
- Big black stat numbers. Grey labels with a small "?" tooltip dot.
- Purple only for: active nav, primary buttons, the support bubble.
- Icons are line icons, thin stroke. Matches the Lucide rule.
- Type looks like Inter throughout. Headings semibold, not heavy.
