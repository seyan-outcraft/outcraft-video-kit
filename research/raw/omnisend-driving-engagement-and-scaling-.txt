Challenges: Scaling Outreach & Capitalizing on Lead Intent
As a fast-growing B2B SaaS platform, Omnisend processes a high volume of inbound leads, trial signups, and demo requests daily. However, managing this influx manually presented significant challenges. The sales team faced hurdles in consistently following up with varied lead tiers - ranging from smaller Product Qualified Leads (PQLs) to larger enterprise accounts.
Additionally, common SaaS pain points like "Demo No-Shows," uncontacted historical leads, and immediate routing for "Have someone call me" requests were causing potential revenue to slip through the cracks. Relying solely on traditional, manual follow-up was not only labor-intensive but also costly, diverting high-value Sales Development Representatives (SDRs) from closing conversations. Omnisend needed a way to automate and scale their first-touch and follow-up outreach without losing the personalized, urgent feel necessary for B2B conversions.
Solution: Autonomous Revenue Agents by Outcraft AI
To maximize lead conversion and pipeline efficiency, Omnisend deployed Outcraft AI's autonomous conversational agents. Outcraft AI was configured to act as an always-on B2B sales assistant, strategically targeting different stages of the customer journey:
For high-value but difficult-to-reach segments like Demo No-Shows and direct callback requests, Outcraft AI engaged leads immediately. By employing a natural-language AI, the system could quickly determine lead intent, navigate step guesses, and drive the prospect back to a booked meeting or a successful handoff.
Activating Scale & Dormant Pipeline
For high-volume segments, such as large batches of previously uncontacted leads and diverse PQL brackets, Outcraft AI utilized a highly effective multi-channel approach. The AI dynamically balanced outreach across direct voice calls, SMS, and email. This ensured that thousands of potential customers received personalized touches - whether a helpful product adoption nudge or a direct link to upgrade - without overwhelming the Omnisend sales staff.
Since integrating Outcraft AI into their lead management workflow, Omnisend has achieved substantial improvements across multiple campaign types, directly impacting pipeline health and operational efficiency.
 The most significant business impact has been a steady, reliable flow of sales conversations. Over just three months, Outcraft AI successfully generated 30+ booked meetings per month. This predictable pipeline injection allows the sales team to focus entirely on high-leverage closing activities rather than top-of-funnel chasing.
Exceptional Campaign Engagement:
 Across thousands of leads, Outcraft maintained robust engagement rates well above SaaS industry standards. In specialized, high-intent campaigns (such as rescuing no-shows), engagement skyrocketed to 75%, effectively reviving dormant opportunities. Broader PQL outreach campaigns routinely saw engagement rates hovering near 40%.
Cost-Efficient Conversions at Scale:
 Outcraft AI proved to be an incredibly lean solution. It autonomously managed thousands of leads across various campaigns. The AI successfully secured a reliable stream of positive replies and conversions—particularly excelling in niche recovery campaigns where conversion rates hit an impressive 25%, adding immediate value to the pipeline with minimal operational overhead.
Multi-Channel Synergy & High Deliverability:
 The AI seamlessly orchestrated outreach via calls, emails, and SMS texts. By leveraging this varied touchpoint strategy, email open rates consistently averaged over 50%, while SMS delivery rates held strong at roughly 80%. This ensured that messaging actually reached the decision-makers.
Rather than letting high-volume or "hard-to-reach" leads decay, Omnisend utilized Outcraft to continuously filter, engage, and convert prospects. The system operated flawlessly in the background, proving that AI-driven, multi-channel outreach is a powerful lever for SaaS growth and predictable meeting generation.
