# BRIEF — Video 4: THE UNASKED QUESTION

## What Seyan asked for

A **completely new** video, built in **HyperFrames**. New components, new story,
nothing reused from videos 1–3. An **explainer**, aimed at **B2C / e-commerce**.
It has to carry the psychology, the speed and the pitch. Animations and
transitions should go further than anything built so far. He does not want to be
asked questions — deliver the finished thing.

## The spine — a REFRAME, not problem/rescue

Three angles were rejected before because they all shared one shape:
*problem, then rescue* (see `research/HANDOFF.md`). This one is a different
shape on purpose. It is a **reframe**: take one belief the viewer already holds,
turn it over, and make everything after that follow from the new reading.

> **The belief:** an abandoned cart is a lost sale.
> **The turn:** it isn't. It's a question nobody answered.
> **The consequence:** so answer it. Out loud. In minutes.

The line is Outcraft's own B2C hero copy — *"An abandoned cart isn't a lost
sale. It's a question waiting to be asked."* — which means the spine is the
brand's own positioning, not an invention.

## Why this is the right story for B2C

E-commerce is the half of Outcraft's business with the **hardest numbers** and
the **most human psychology**. The B2B story is pipeline. The B2C story is a
person, alone, at 11pm, with a full cart and one unanswered question. That is a
film. Pipeline is not.

## What the viewer must feel

1. **Recognition** — "that is my checkout."
2. **A jolt** — the reframe has to land as a genuine turn, not a slogan.
3. **Relief** — the questions finally get answered.
4. **Greed** — the numbers are real and they are large.

## Non-negotiables

- 48.0s, 120 BPM, 24 bars, 30fps. Never faster than 120 BPM (DO-NOT-DO 40).
- Music + sound design. **No voiceover** — matches videos 1, 2 and 3.
- Three renders every time: music+SFX, SFX-only, silent.
- Every number on screen traces to `research/facts.json` with a source.
- Brand kit copied in, never re-invented.
- Never shake the frame (DO-NOT-DO 38). Centre the type (39).
- One CTA at the end, logo last (DO-NOT-DO 29, 34).
- No emoji, no stock people, no naming competitors on screen.
