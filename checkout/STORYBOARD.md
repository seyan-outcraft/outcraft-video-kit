# VIDEO 4 — THE UNASKED QUESTION

48.0s · 1440 frames · 120 BPM · 24 bars · 1920x1080 · HyperFrames

A beat is 0.5s. A bar is 2.0s. Every scene start and every cut lands on a beat.

---

## The spine

Not problem-then-rescue. That shape was rejected three times (`research/HANDOFF.md`).
This one is a **reframe** — one belief, turned over, and everything follows from
the new reading.

> **The belief:** an abandoned cart is a lost sale.
> **The turn:** it isn't. It's a question nobody answered.
> **The consequence:** so answer it. Out loud. In minutes.

The turn is Outcraft's own B2C hero line (`pos.b2c_hero`), so the spine is the
brand's own positioning rather than an invention.

The film closes its own loop: the **$284.00** cart that dies at 3.0s comes back
alive at 30.6s. Somebody who watches only the first four seconds and the last
eight still gets the whole proposition.

### What the hold-time pass changed

Every headline was measured from the frame it finishes landing to the frame the
cut takes it away. Three came in under the 1.5s a line needs to be readable
(DO-NOT-DO 26), and all three were lines the film cannot afford to lose:

| Line | Was | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `IT ISN'T.` | 0.80s | **1.71s** |
| `QUESTION.` | 1.17s | **1.43s** |
| `NOBODY ASKED.` | 1.08s | **1.52s** |

The half second came out of scene 6, the email shot — the one shot in the film
whose job is to feel like nothing is happening, and therefore the only one that
gets shorter without losing anything.

## Scene detail

### s1 — THE CART · 0.0–4.0 · cream

Eyebrow `CHECKOUT` top-centre, tracked out, arrives 0.2.
Hero: a total counting **$0.00 → $284.00** on an odometer roll, centred, 260px,
width reserved from frame one (DO-NOT-DO 56 — a growing centred string walks).
Roll finishes 2.4. Beat.
3.0 — the number desaturates to `inkMuted`, a coral rule strikes through it,
and `ABANDONED` types in beneath in small caps, coral.
**Motion:** `counterRoll` on the digits, `strikeThrough` on the rule.

### s2 — SEVENTY · 4.0–7.0 · cream

**Cut 1 — DIGIT DROP.** The seven glyphs of `$284.00` drop out of the bottom of
the frame under gravity, staggered right-to-left, while `70%` rises up through
the gap they leave.

Small `ABOUT` above. Hero **`70%`** at 400px. Under it, `OF CARTS END LIKE THIS.`
Fact: `stat.abandonment` — verified against Baymard Institute (70.22%, 50 studies).
**Motion:** `dropIn` out, `riseMask` in.

### s3 — THE BELIEF · 7.0–9.5 · near-black

**Cut 2 — STRIKE AND INVERT.** A coral rule draws left-to-right through the 70%,
and as it completes the whole frame inverts to near-black in one beat.

Eyebrow `YOU CALL THIS`. Hero **`A LOST SALE.`** at 200px, white, centred.
**Motion:** `riseMask` per word, 0.1s stagger.

### s4 — THE TURN · 9.5–15.0 · black → purple

**Cut 3 — SPLIT SHEAR.** The frame splits on the baseline of `A LOST SALE.`;
the top half shears left and the bottom half shears right, and they carry the
words off with them. This is the most violent move in the film, and it is the
one it is built around.

- 9.5–11.9 — **`IT ISN'T.`** 260px, white on black. Lands 10.19, holds 1.71s.
- 12.0 — ground floods to `purple` from the centre outward.
- 12.15–14.55 — **`IT'S A`** / **`QUESTION.`** two lines, 260px, white on purple.
  `QUESTION.` arrives one beat after `IT'S A`, from below, through a mask.

Fact: `pos.b2c_hero`. Two brand colours in the shot, one filled purple element.
**Motion:** `riseMask`, then `floodOut` on the ground.

### s5 — THE QUESTIONS · 15.0–21.0 · cream

**Cut 4 — GLYPH IRIS.** A giant `?` lands on the purple, and then its DOT opens
out and becomes the window onto the cream ground. It was drawn through the
counter of the glyph at first — but a question mark has no counter. It has a
dot, and that is the only closed shape in the character.

Four real shopper questions arrive one per bar and **stay** (DO-NOT-DO 42 —
nothing vanishes to make room; the stack only ever grows):

1. 15.12 — `Will it fit?`
2. 15.92 — `When does it ship?`
3. 16.72 — `Is it really titanium?`
4. 17.52 — `Can I send it back?`

Each bubble carries a `…` that pulses three times and never resolves.
18.5 — the stack lifts and steps down to 0.82 scale, and **`NOBODY ASKED.`**
lands in the space it opened, 150px, coral. Nothing fades out and nothing is
removed: hierarchy comes from size and position (DO-NOT-DO 42).

Grounded, not invented: Goth N' Rock's own case study names sizing and shipping
as the questions that stop a checkout, and Taima sells titanium cookware.
**Motion:** `scatterIn` per bubble, `pulse` on the ellipsis.

### s6 — THE EMAIL · 21.0–24.0 · light grey

**Cut 5 — VACUUM.** All four bubbles and the headline converge to a single point
at the centre of the frame and are swallowed. The point becomes an envelope.

One small envelope, alone, centre-left, on a wide empty ground. A clock hand
sweeps. Hero on the right: **`5%`** at 340px, `inkMuted`.
Caption: `is what it gets back.`
Fact: `stat.phone_vs_email` — used as a **comparison**, which is what its risk
note permits, and its other half lands in s9.
**Motion:** `riseMask`, deliberately slow. This is the only sleepy shot in the film.

### s7 — SO ASK THEM · 24.0–28.0 · deep indigo

**Cut 6 — RING WIPE.** Three concentric rings expand from the envelope at the
speed of a phone ringing, and the third one carries the indigo ground with it
to the edges of the frame.

Hero **`SO ASK THEM.`** 220px, white, centred — the direct answer to s4.
Under it a live voice waveform starts drawing, 64 bars, purple.
Small: `VOICE · SMS · WHATSAPP · EMAIL` (`pos.channels`, approved).
**Motion:** `riseMask` on the line, `waveDraw` on the waveform.

### s8 — ANSWERED · 28.0–32.5 · dark teal

**Cut 7 — WAVEFORM WIPE.** The waveform itself is the wipe edge: it travels
right and everything behind it is the new shot. The wipe is the sound.

28.4–30.0 — the four questions from s5 return as a tight column and each takes a
purple tick, one per 0.4s, in the order they were asked.
30.6 — **`$284.00`** returns, alive, purple, at the size it died at — the same
digits, the same place. Under it: `RECOVERED.`
This is the loop closing. It is the single most important frame in the film.
**Motion:** `tickIn` per row, `counterRoll` reversed on the total.

### s9 — THE CHAIN · 32.5–36.25 · cream

**Cut 8 — CARD FLIP.** The recovered-total tile rotates 180° on its Y axis and

### s10 — AGAIN · 36.5–40.5 · purple

| 3 | 9.50 | Split shear — the frame halves shear opposite ways, carrying the words |
| 4 | 14.55 | Glyph iris — the DOT of a `?` opens out onto the next shot |
| 5 | 20.80 | Vacuum — everything converges to one point and is swallowed |
| 6 | 23.50 | Ring wipe — three phone-ring rings, the third carrying the indigo out |
| 7 | 27.62 | Waveform wipe — a ragged edge cut to the voice is the wipe |

| 9 | 36.20 | Diagonal tear — cream slides apart on a seam, revealing purple |
| 10 | 40.20 | Count blur — a black bar opens across the frame and clears |

**A cut cannot be made out of the colour the frame is already wearing**
(DO-NOT-DO 62). Four of these were built that way at first and were literally
invisible on a rendered still: a teal card flipping on teal, a purple tear
opening onto purple, a purple bar on purple, and an indigo ring arriving after
the ground had already gone indigo. Each one now moves against the colour it is
replacing, not the colour it is becoming.

## Video direction

**Grounds.** Cream `#FAF8F1` is home. It goes dark exactly three times — s3/s4
for the turn, s8 for the answer, s11 for the money — and comes home for the
close. Purple is spent on the two moments that matter: the reframe (s4) and the
second call (s10).

**Type.** Bricolage Grotesque 700 for every headline, Inter for every label.
Never below 150px on a headline, never below 78px on support. Every headline is
measured against the 1636px safe width at ~0.55em per character before it is
believed (DO-NOT-DO 67), and every hero box carries `white-space: nowrap` so an
overflow is visible instead of a silent reflow.

**Motion doctrine.** Things that enter decelerate; things that leave accelerate;
no linear easing anywhere. The frame never shakes (DO-NOT-DO 38). The camera
never moves. Type lands on a bezier curve and stops exactly, never on a spring
(DO-NOT-DO 53) — and once a move is over its transform is dropped entirely, not
zeroed (54).

**Nothing vanishes to make room.** The question stack in s5, the tick column in
s8 and the proof rows in s11 only ever grow. Hierarchy comes from size and
position (DO-NOT-DO 42).

**Colour discipline.** Two brand colours per shot, one filled purple element per
frame, coral only ever small — the strike in s1, `NOBODY ASKED.` in s5.

**Sound.** One frame-pinned cue table in `tools/cues.mjs`, no audio in any scene.
The bed is `assets/audio/bed-48.mp3` — the 120 BPM Mixkit tech-house bed, already
rebuilt to 48.0s on a downbeat for video 2. Vocabulary is whoosh / impact /
riser / sparkle / transition plus real foley; never a synth bleep (DO-NOT-DO 50).
Three renders ship every time (51).
